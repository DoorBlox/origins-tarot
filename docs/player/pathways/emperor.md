## Permanent weakness

**Weight of the Crown:** Holding a shield slows movement by 25%; sprinting costs 50% more exhaustion.

This is attached to your original Origin and persists when you edit Sequence slots. Read the individual ability conditions below for other situational penalties.

## Using your keys and Focus

Configure Primary/Secondary Active Power in Options → Controls → Key Binds → Origins. G and H are suggestions, not fixed keys. Use `/trigger a_focus_menu` → choose an equipped slot → open its Focus book → click an active ability → Select this ability. Passives activate without a key. [Detailed controls](../CONTROLS.md).
