## How acting marks work

Acting alternates **ITEM → EVENT → ITEM** for your permanent *primary* pathway even if your partner is unlocked. An item proof means having the listed item in inventory; it does not consume it. Each accepted proof gives one mark and starts a **2,400 tick (120 second)** cooldown. Run `/trigger a_roadmap` to see the next task, timer and ritual requirements.

## How to ascend

Stand **on a lectern with crying obsidian directly beneath it**, be grounded (not gliding), out of combat for **200 ticks / 10 seconds**, carry the correct offerings, and have sufficient acting marks. Then type `/trigger a_ascend`. Successful rituals consume the listed offerings; locked Sequence 1 does not.

Sequence 1, Sequence 0 and the corresponding Resonance suites remain unavailable via ordinary play. Do not try the operator-only developer preview on a regular SMP.
