## Your first five minutes

1. Join a **Minecraft Java 1.18.2 Arcane Rev3** test server and choose a Tarot Origin. You start at **Sequence 9**.
2. Type **/trigger a_book**. Put the Arcane Handbook in your hand and **right-click**. Its blue links jump to sections and all 22 Origins.
3. Type **/trigger a_tome**. Right-click the Pathway Tome, click a Sequence in its contents, then click any ability to read **what it does** and **how to use it**.
4. Go to **Esc → Options → Controls → Key Binds → Origins** and bind **Primary Active Power** and **Secondary Active Power**. G and H are just suggestions.
5. If abilities share keys, use **/trigger a_focus_menu**. It gives an interactive Focus book. Choose an equipped slot, open the resulting kit book, choose a named ability, and click **Select this ability**.
6. Use **/trigger a_roadmap** to see your next acting item/event and cooldown. Proofs alternate **ITEM → EVENT** with a 120-second cooldown between accepted proofs.
7. Once you have the required marks and offerings, ascend at the lectern-on-crying-obsidian altar. See [the complete ritual chart](ASCENSION.md).

## Example: The Fool

At Sequence 9, the Fool has **Spirit Vision**. Bind Secondary Active Power (for example, H), focus Spirit Vision if needed, then **sneak + press your Secondary key**. Entities within 10 blocks get Glowing for 2 seconds. Cooldown: 5 seconds.

Your permanent drawback, **Spiritual Expenditure**, makes sprinting and Elytra flight use **25% more exhaustion**.

## What is actually unlocked?

Sequences **9–5** equip primary pathway kits automatically. At **Sequence 4**, your fixed second pathway unlocks and you get six editable slots. Sequence 3 gives seven; Sequence 2 gives eight. **Each slot equips one entire pathway-and-Sequence kit**, not one ability. Normal Sequence 1/0 and Resonance cannot yet be unlocked.

The [linked pathway reference](PATHWAYS.md) includes skills at higher normal Sequences for reading, but that does **not** mean your character already owns them.

## What version am I playing?

This manual describes **Arcane V2 Rev3** and its own **a_** trigger commands. The older Tarot V2/V3 packs and **tc_** commands are separate systems. Your SMP **does not update automatically when GitHub code is merged**. If a new book is missing or chat still overflows, your administrator should consult the [safe installation guide](SERVER_SETUP.md).
